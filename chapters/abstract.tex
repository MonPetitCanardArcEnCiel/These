    \textbf{Abstract}

With the rapid development of radar technologies and auto-guided weapon system, modern fighter aircraft increasingly need to take stealth into account, while retaining their aerodynamic qualities. Thus, modern aircraft design methods have to accomodate requirements for low observability, aerodynamic stability and efficiency, and maneuverability that share complex relations and are often antagonistic. To that end, multiple frameworks are being developped, under the name of MultiDisciplinary Optimization.
